About Me

I am a medical physics researcher in the Division of Medical Physics, Department of Radiation Oncology at Stanford Medicine. My research focuses on multi-modal, physician-guided auto-segmentation and radiotherapy treatment planning. I am also completing the CAMPEP Medical Physics Certificate Program at Stanford, on the path to board certification as a clinical medical physicist. I hold a PhD in Physics from Caltech, where I worked on quantum gravity and stochastic modeling. My background also includes reinforcement learning for robot navigation and quantitative modeling at Goldman Sachs.

Outside of research, I am an avid wildlife and landscape photographer. Through my lens, I seek to capture the beauty and biodiversity of the natural world, telling stories that highlight both its fragility and resilience. Photography allows me to combine a precise, analytical mindset with artistic expression, reflecting my curiosity and appreciation for the connection between science, nature, and storytelling.
